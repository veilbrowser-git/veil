FAQ

Questions

Is Veil free?

Yes. Veil, the VPN (on the Tor network), Veil Pass, Aegis and the image creator are free, with no account. Pro ($2.99/mo) and Ultra ($5.99/mo) add deeper AI and a faster VPN.

Does Aegis send my data anywhere?

No. Aegis runs on your Mac. Your questions, the pages it reads and the files you give it never leave your computer. When web search is on, it looks up your question on a public encyclopedia to find sources, the same as you visiting it.

Do I need an API key or an AI subscription?

No. Aegis downloads its model once (about 2.5 GB on computers, 1.1 GB on Android) and then runs offline. The optional image creator is a one-time 4.2 GB download.

Which devices does Veil run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), Linux (64-bit) and Android 10+ phones. Intel Macs and iPhone aren't supported yet. Aegis runs best on computers with 8 GB of memory or more; on Android it uses a smaller model sized for phones.

How does the Veil VPN work?

Everything you browse in Veil goes through an encrypted connection (the Tor network, Cloudflare WARP, or a Veil server), hiding your IP address and location from the sites you visit and from the Wi-Fi network you're on. If the connection drops, Veil stops loading pages instead of leaking. It protects browsing in Veil, not other apps. The VPN is free on every plan through the Tor network, with no account (it's slower than a paid server); Pro and Ultra add a fast location through Cloudflare WARP (install Cloudflare's free 1.1.1.1 app once), and Ultra adds every location. It's on Mac, Windows and Linux; Android support is coming.

Is Veil Pass safe?

Your passwords are encrypted on your device with your master password and never sent anywhere. Veil only fills a password on the exact website it was saved for. Because nobody else has your master password, it can't be recovered if you forget it.

Is Veil a Chrome extension?

No. Veil is a full browser built on the same engine as Chrome (Chromium), so websites work the way you expect. Download it here for Mac, Windows, Linux or Android and open it like any other app.

Why does my device warn me the first time I open Veil?

Veil isn't in the App Store, Microsoft Store or Play Store yet, so your device asks you to confirm. On Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On Windows: "More info" → "Run anyway". On Android: allow installing apps from your browser when asked.
